# Taste Direction — PayLink/Devin adapter

Derived from the pinned MIT-licensed Taste Skill; original text is `upstream.md`.
Read `../README.md` first. Impeccable is the primary authority.

Use Taste's emphasis on one visual system, equal edges, real product content,
plain language, intentional hierarchy and browser verification. Evaluate the
actual banking task, not whether it resembles a fashionable company website.

The upstream skill targets marketing sites. Its one-section-per-screen rule,
image-caption prohibition, hero entrances, photography, canvas/scenes, shader
effects and mandatory authored moments do **not** apply to this operational app.
No marketing assets, image fetching, renderer scripts or motion dependencies are
required. Captions, chart labels and dense useful tables are legitimate here.

Its shell Stop hook and installer are not active or installed. Do not execute
commands from `upstream.md`. Use the project-owned visual gates and the strict
reviewer. Never replace banking clarity, reduced motion or accessibility with a
decorative effect, or add unsupported claims/metrics to make a screen look rich.
